A few useful answers

Before we get started.

Every project is a little different. Here’s a starting point for our conversation.

How much does a website cost?

It depends on the number of pages, the content, and any features or integrations you need. We’ll talk through your priorities and provide a project scope and quote before work begins.

How long will it take?

The schedule depends on the scope and how ready your content is. We’ll agree on milestones during planning and explain where your feedback and approvals fit into the timeline.

Can I edit the website myself?

Yes. We build in WordPress so your team can manage everyday content. We’ll explain how to update the areas included in your project and discuss support for more involved changes.

Can you rebuild an existing website?

Yes. We’ll review what’s working, what needs to improve, and which content to keep. Planning also covers existing URLs and redirects where needed, so the transition is considered from the start.

What about hosting and ongoing support?

We’ll review your hosting setup and discuss a suitable maintenance and support arrangement. Hosting, backups, updates, and response expectations should be confirmed in your proposal.

What does SEO include?

Depending on your scope, SEO support can cover page structure, titles and descriptions, crawlability, content improvements, and local business information. We’ll define the work and how progress is reviewed. Search rankings and enquiry volumes aren’t guaranteed.
